# Hey, I'm Landon Kea 👋

I collect ideas, skills, hobbies, unfinished side projects, obscure facts, random adventures, and the occasional existential crisis after opening 247 browser tabs that all seemed important at the time.

Curiosity isn't just part of my personality — it's practically a full-time job.

If something catches my attention, there's a decent chance I'll disappear down a rabbit hole and emerge several hours later knowing how suspension bridges are engineered, why octopuses solve puzzles for fun, the history of abandoned amusement parks, and absolutely nothing about the thing I originally meant to research.

I wouldn't have it any other way.

---

## So... who is Landon?

I'm someone who believes life is way too interesting to stay in just one lane.

I love building things, solving problems, creating ideas, learning skills nobody asked me to learn, and asking *"What happens if...?"* and *"Why does this happen...?"* far more often than most people probably should.

Whether it's designing something, creating content, solving a technical challenge, helping people, learning an entirely new piece of software just because it exists, or diving headfirst into an ambitious project that probably started as *"this should only take five to ten minutes"* — I'm happiest when I'm building something meaningful. (This is so very true as of recent with the love of my life.)

I don't chase perfection. I chase progress. And occasionally dopamine.

## Things I Actually Enjoy (Which Explain... A Lot)

My hobbies make perfect sense to exactly two groups of people:

- People who crave novelty.
- And people who have accepted that curiosity is a lifestyle.

You'll probably find me...

- Falling into obscure research rabbit holes because one random question deserved an answer.
- Teaching myself completely unnecessary skills simply because they looked fun.
- Exploring forgotten roads, abandoned places, hidden overlooks, or anywhere that makes people ask, *"How did you even find this?"*
- Taking spontaneous road trips with no destination other than *"that direction looks interesting."*
- Photographing storms, unusual architecture, weird signs, old machinery, forgotten places, and anything that tells a story.
- Building ambitious side projects before my last ambitious side project has emotionally recovered.
- Touching a thing I've never touched just to see if I can figure it out in a couple of weekends.
- Collecting random skills instead of random stuff.
- Hiking the trail everyone else avoided because *"too difficult"* sounded like a recommendation.
- Visiting museums, observatories, factories, or places where I can leave knowing something I didn't when I walked in.
- Turning ordinary days into mini expeditions.
- Hyper-fixating on something until I accidentally become *"the person who knows way too much about that."*
- Constantly redesigning systems to organize my life because designing systems is somehow more fun than using them.
- Collecting experiences instead of souvenirs.
- Beginning entirely too many sentences with, *"You know what would be neat (or cool)?"*
